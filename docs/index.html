A note from the curator

History isn’t a straight line
from wrong to right.

This is a museum of unfinished possibilities, not a scoreboard of failures. Concorde carried passengers for decades. Xanadu remains an alternative vision. Their differences matter.

Every exhibit links to its sources. The “what if?” room is interpretation, clearly marked. The drawings are original impressions of the objects, not historical plans.
